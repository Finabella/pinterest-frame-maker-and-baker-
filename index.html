About Me

Hi! I'm Fina, a passionate creator from the University of Bamenda, Cameroon. I blend two artistic passions into every project: visual frame design and artisan baking.

Each frame I create tells a story through colors, textures, and artistic expression. Whether it's vibrant digital designs or traditional painted frames, I approach every project with meticulous attention to detail.

My baking creations are more than just treats—they're edible art. From delicate pastries to stunning celebration cakes, I combine flavors with visual artistry to create memorable experiences.
